# Privacy Policy

The developer of "Learn Taiwan Mandarin" (Japanese name: まいにち台湾華語; the "App") handles information about users of the App in accordance with this Privacy Policy.

Effective date: (insert the publication date)

## 1. Information we collect

1. **Account information**
   - The App automatically creates an anonymous account the first time you open it. You do not need to register your name or email address.
   - If you sign in with a Google account, we receive your email address, name, and profile photo from Google.
2. **Profile and settings**
   - Your nickname and icon image (set by you; if you sign in with Google and have not set them, your Google name and photo are used as the initial values).
   - Settings such as display language, ruby display, daily study time, skills to study, study reminder time, and voice for audio.
3. **Learning records**
   - Your answers to questions, review schedule, XP earned and study streak, favorite questions, and placement test results.
4. **Question reports**
   - When you report an error in a question, the reason and your comment (up to 500 characters).
5. **Voice**
   - Your voice when you speak into the microphone in speaking questions. The App converts it to text using your device's speech recognition and uses only the result for scoring. We do not receive the audio itself (see "Device speech recognition" in Section 3).

The App does not use advertising or third-party analytics services. We do not collect location, contacts, or device identifiers (such as advertising IDs).

## 2. How we use information

- To save your learning records and to schedule reviews and choose questions suited to you.
- To carry over your learning records across devices and when you sign in with Google.
- To analyze your weak areas and create a study plan from your placement test results.
- To fix errors in the learning materials and improve the App based on reports.
- To respond to inquiries.

## 3. Third-party services

The App uses the following services and sends them only the information they need. These providers are located in the United States. Please see each provider's privacy policy for how they handle information.

| Service | Provider | Information sent | Purpose |
|---|---|---|---|
| Supabase | Supabase, Inc. (US) | Information in 1.1–1.4 | Data storage (server location: Japan (Tokyo)) |
| Google Sign-In | Google LLC (US) | Sign-in with your Google account | Sign-in (only if you choose it) |
| Claude API | Anthropic, PBC (US) | Placement test scores by level and skill, and the questions you got wrong | Weakness analysis and study plan |
| GitHub | GitHub, Inc. (US) | Report reason and comment, and information about the reported question | Managing reports |
| Resend | Resend, Inc. (US) | Report reason and comment, and information about the reported question | Notifying the developer of reports |

- We do not send your name, email address, or account ID to the Claude API, GitHub, or Resend.
- Except as required by law, we do not provide your information to any other third party without your consent.

**Device speech recognition**: Audio for speaking questions is processed by your device's speech recognition (e.g. Android). Depending on your device and its settings, the audio may be sent to and processed on servers of the device manufacturer or OS provider (such as Google), in accordance with their privacy policies.

**Audio for learning materials**: The audio for the learning materials is generated in advance by the developer and is not created from your information.

## 4. Information stored on your device

So that you can study offline, the App stores learning materials, their audio, your settings, and part of your learning records on your device. Uninstalling the App deletes this information from your device.

## 5. Retention and deletion

- We keep your information for as long as your account exists.
- **Reset learning data**: On the "Account" screen in the App, you can delete your learning records (answers, review schedule, favorites, and placement test results).
- **Delete account**: On the "Account" screen in the App, you can delete your account. This deletes all information in 1.1–1.4 from our servers.
- Reports that have already been delivered to the developer (records on GitHub and notification emails) are kept to improve the learning materials. They do not include your name or account ID.

## 6. Security

We take measures such as encrypted communication and access controls that allow only you to read and write your own data, to protect your information from leakage, loss, or alteration.

## 7. Children

The App is not primarily directed at children under 13. Children under 13 should use the App only with the consent of a parent or guardian.

## 8. Your rights

You can view, change, and delete your information in the App. For other requests, such as disclosure, correction, or suspension of use, please contact us using the information in Section 10.

## 9. Changes to this policy

We may change this policy as needed. We will notify you in the App or on this page of any significant changes.
